🇮🇹 Example: Italian work authorization documents 📄

1️⃣ Receipt - the employer's application in the Italian Ministry of Interior system
2️⃣ Letter from the Immigration Office (Sportello Unico) - sent to the Italian Embassy in New Delhi, after which the worker applies for the visa 🛂

⚠️ Know what is real:
In Italy the process ALWAYS starts with the employer's application. No application = no work visa. Anyone promising "Italy visa in 2 weeks" without it is lying ❌

Пример визы Польша

Файлы: visa_pl.jpg
🇵🇱 Example: Polish national work visa (type D) 🛂

How to recognise a real one:
✔️ It is a sticker in the passport, not a paper printout
✔️ Type "D" - national visa
✔️ Issued by the Polish consulate
✔️ Purpose of issue ("CEL WYDANIA") printed on it

⚠️ Be careful: a Schengen tourist visa is NOT a work visa. Anyone selling "work on a tourist visa" is putting you at risk ❌
